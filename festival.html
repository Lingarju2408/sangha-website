About The Festival

Ten days of devotion and celebration

Gowri Ganesha is the most cherished festival on the Sangha's calendar. Every year, our youth come together to install Lord Ganesha, perform the Gowri Pooja, and organize daily aarti, bhajans, cultural competitions and prasada distribution — bringing the whole village of Sidhihosakote together in celebration.

From the Ganesh Sthapana to the final Visarjana procession, every ritual and program is organized entirely by our young volunteers, guided by our elders and priests.

Festival Timeline

Dates and timings for this year's Gowri Ganesha celebration.

Day 1 · 12 Sep, 6:00 AM

Ganesh Sthapana

Sacred installation of Lord Ganesha at the Sangha mandapa with Vedic rituals.

Day 1 · 12 Sep, 5:00 PM

Gowri Pooja

Traditional Gowri Pooja performed by the women of the village, followed by community aarti.

Day 2–8 · Daily, 7:00 AM & 7:30 PM

Daily Aarti & Bhajans

Morning and evening aarti with devotional bhajan sessions led by youth and elders.

Day 5 · 16 Sep, 6:00 PM

Cultural Programs & Competitions

Dance, drama, singing and drawing competitions for children and youth, with prizes for winners.

Day 7 · 18 Sep, 12:00 PM

Prasada Distribution

Community lunch and prasada distribution for all visiting devotees and families.

Day 10 · 21 Sep, 4:00 PM

Ganesh Visarjana

Grand procession through Sidhihosakote with music and dance, concluding at the village pond for Visarjana.

What Happens During The Festival

👻

Ganesh Sthapana

The ceremonial installation that marks the beginning of the ten-day celebration.

🌹

Gowri Pooja

A revered ritual honoring Goddess Gowri, performed with full traditional rites.

🔥

Daily Aarti

Morning and evening aarti open to all devotees of the village.

🎵

Bhajans

Devotional singing sessions that bring together families each evening.

🎭

Cultural Programs

Dance, drama and competitions showcasing local youth talent.

🍴

Prasada Distribution

Community meals shared with everyone who visits during the festival.

🏆

Competitions

Fun contests for children and adults with festival-themed prizes.

🤝

Community Activities

Volunteer-run stalls, decoration and coordination activities throughout.

🌊

Visarjana

The closing procession and immersion ceremony that concludes the festival.
