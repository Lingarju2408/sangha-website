Who We Are

A Sangha rooted in tradition, run by the youth

Chikkuru Bagilu Jai Shri Ram Yuvakara Sangha is a youth-led community organization based in Sidhihosakote, Karnataka. Formed by a group of local young people who wanted to protect and celebrate our village's traditions, the Sangha has grown into the heart of religious, cultural and social life in Sidhihosakote.

We organize the annual Gowri Ganesha festival, Shri Ram Navami celebrations, cultural programs, sports events and a wide range of social service activities — always led by youth, for the community.

💎

Our History

Founded by a small group of Sidhihosakote youth to keep the Gowri Ganesha tradition and village festivals alive, the Sangha has since expanded into social service and youth development, growing year after year with community support.

🎯

Our Vision

A united, self-reliant Sidhihosakote where every young person actively carries forward Sanatana Dharma while contributing to the welfare of the community.

📌

Our Mission

To organize meaningful religious, cultural and social programs; to develop youth leadership; and to serve the people of Sidhihosakote with dedication and humility.

Community Development

Our Objectives

  • Organize the Gowri Ganesha festival and other religious observances each year
  • Preserve and promote local Sanatana Dharma traditions and Kannada culture
  • Provide a platform for youth leadership, sports and skill development
  • Undertake regular social service activities for Sidhihosakote and nearby villages
  • Build unity among the youth of the village across all backgrounds

Community & Youth Initiatives

  • Annual blood donation camps and health check-up drives
  • Village cleanliness and tree plantation programs
  • Educational support and scholarships for deserving students
  • Sports tournaments and fitness activities for local youth
  • Bhajan classes, cultural training and festival volunteering
