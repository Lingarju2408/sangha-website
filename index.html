Who We Are

A home for youth, faith and community

Chikkuru Bagilu Jai Shri Ram Yuvakara Sangha brings together young people of Sidhihosakote to organize festivals, protect local traditions, and serve the community — from the Gowri Ganesha celebration each year to blood donation camps, cleanliness drives and cultural programs that keep our Sanatana heritage alive for the next generation.

🔥

Gowri Ganesha Festival

Our biggest celebration of the year — Ganesh Sthapana, Gowri Pooja, daily aarti, bhajans and community feasts.

🏴

Shri Ram & Dharma Activities

Ram Navami celebrations, satsangs, and observances that keep our village rooted in Sanatana Dharma.

🎭

Cultural Programs

Folk dance, devotional music, drama and competitions that showcase local talent and tradition.

🤝

Social Service

Blood donation camps, food distribution, tree plantation and help during emergencies for our village.

🏆

Sports & Youth Activities

Tournaments, fitness drives and youth-led activities that build unity and healthy competition.

📣

Latest Announcements

Meeting dates, volunteer calls and festival updates — stay connected with everything the Sangha is doing.
